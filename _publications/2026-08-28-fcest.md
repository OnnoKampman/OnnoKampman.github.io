FCEst is a Python package for estimating static and time-varying functional connectivity (TVFC) in fMRI data.
It implements the Wishart process approach introduced in [Time-varying functional connectivity as Wishart processes](/publication/2024-06-01-time-varying-functional), alongside established baselines including DCC and GO MGARCH models and sliding-window estimators, within a common benchmarking framework.
